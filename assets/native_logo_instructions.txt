Native launch image instructions

Android:
- The Android launch screen uses the native launcher icon referenced in `android/app/src/main/res/drawable/launch_background.xml` and `drawable-v21/launch_background.xml`.
- To make your logo appear on the Android loading screen and launcher, replace the mipmap launcher icons under:
  - `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-mdpi/ic_launcher.png`
  - `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-hdpi/ic_launcher.png`
  - `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-xhdpi/ic_launcher.png`
  - `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-xxhdpi/ic_launcher.png`
  - `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png`
- You can generate properly sized launcher icons using `flutter_launcher_icons` or an online tool and copy them into the mipmap folders.

iOS:
- The iOS launch screen references an image set named `LaunchImage` (see `ios/Runner/Base.lproj/LaunchScreen.storyboard`).
- Place your launch images into `ios/Runner/Assets.xcassets/LaunchImage.imageset` with filenames matching the entries in `Contents.json`:
  - `LaunchImage.png` (1x)
  - `LaunchImage@2x.png` (2x)
  - `LaunchImage@3x.png` (3x)
- Alternatively, use Xcode's asset catalog and AppIcon tools to generate a proper set.

App icon (launcher icon):
- If you want the app icon (the one users tap to open the app) to be your logo, use `flutter_launcher_icons` or replace the mipmap and iOS AppIcon images in the platform folders.

After copying the images, run:

flutter pub get
flutter build apk
(or `flutter run` to test on device)
